# Client Reimbursement Policy (2026)

## Taxi Expenses

Taxi expenses **over 100 RMB** require **direct manager approval**.
Submit the original invoice, the trip purpose, and the manager's
approval note within 14 days of travel.

## Hotel Expenses

Hotel expenses **over 200 RMB per night** require **both an invoice and
a signed travel approval form**. Stays below the threshold can be
reimbursed with the invoice alone.
