# Beta Catering Ltd. — Invoice Compliance Rule (2026)

> **Fictional client.** Beta Catering Ltd. is a placeholder client used
> for demos.

## Delivery Service Invoices

Delivery service invoices **without a clear service description**
should be **flagged for manual review** before bookkeeping.

Acceptable service descriptions include — but are not limited to:

- `meal delivery`
- `platform service fee`
- `packaging fee`
- `延时配送费`
- `平台技术服务费`

Invoices with vague descriptions (e.g. just "服务费") must NOT be
booked directly. The engagement lead must contact Beta Catering Ltd.
to request a corrected invoice or a written description supplement.

## Notes

This rule is specific to Beta Catering Ltd. and is informed by the
nature of their platform-mediated delivery operations.
