# Client Reimbursement Policy (2024)

## Taxi Expenses

Taxi expenses **under 200 RMB** can be reimbursed without manager
approval. Submit the original invoice and the trip purpose within 30
days of travel.

## Hotel Expenses

Hotel expenses **under 300 RMB per night** can be reimbursed with the
invoice only. No travel approval form is required for stays at or below
this threshold.

## Notes

This is the 2024 version of the firm-wide client reimbursement policy.
It is superseded by `reimbursement_policy_2026.md` for travel that
occurs on or after 2026-01-01.
