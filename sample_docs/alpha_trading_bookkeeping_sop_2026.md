# Alpha Trading Co. — Bookkeeping SOP (2026)

> **Fictional client.** Alpha Trading Co. is a placeholder client used
> for demos. All references to client data in this repository are
> invented.

## Meal & Entertainment Expenses

Meal invoices submitted for **client entertainment** should be recorded
under **business entertainment expenses (业务招待费)**. Two artefacts
are required before the entry is booked:

1. A **valid invoice** matching the firm-name and tax-id requirements.
2. A **signed client visit note** describing the date, the attendees,
   and the business purpose of the meal.

If either artefact is missing, the entry must be flagged for **manual
review** by the engagement lead before posting.

## Notes

This SOP is specific to Alpha Trading Co. It must not be applied to
other clients without explicit cross-engagement review.
